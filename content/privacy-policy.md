## 1. Introduction

Welcome to HeartLoop.

HeartLoop ("HeartLoop," "we," "our," or "us") is an online social discovery and connection platform designed to help people create profiles, discover connections, communicate, and interact through our mobile applications, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using HeartLoop, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent before collecting or processing particular categories of personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information depending on the features you use.

### 2.1 Account and Registration Information

When you register or access HeartLoop, we may collect:

- Mobile phone number
- Email address, if provided
- Name, display name, or username
- Date of birth or age information
- Account credentials and identifiers
- Authentication and account verification records

### 2.2 Profile Information

When you create or update your profile, we may collect:

- Profile photographs and uploaded media
- Biography, interests, and preferences
- Gender and other profile details you choose to provide
- Information you voluntarily share with other users

Some profile information may be visible to other HeartLoop users according to the application's privacy settings and features.

### 2.3 Messages and Interactions

When you communicate or interact through HeartLoop, we may process:

- Messages and associated metadata
- Chat timestamps
- Connection requests and interaction history
- Reports, blocks, and safety-related information
- Other user-generated content

We process this information to provide communication features, maintain functionality, prevent abuse, investigate reports, and enforce platform rules.

### 2.4 Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

- IP address
- Device type and operating system
- App version
- Device identifiers where permitted
- Login timestamps and security events
- Crash reports and diagnostic information
- Technical usage and performance data

### 2.5 Location Information

Where supported and with any necessary permissions, HeartLoop may process location-related information to provide relevant discovery and connection features.

This may include approximate location, selected city or region, or precise device location when a feature requires it and you explicitly permit access.

You may manage device location permissions through your operating system settings.

### 2.6 Payments and Rewards

Where payment, subscription, coin, reward, or promotional features are available, we may process transaction identifiers, purchase records, balances, redemption activity, and related information.

Payment transactions may be processed by authorized payment service providers. The information handled by those providers is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may process personal information to:

1. Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.

3. Provide profile, discovery, matching, and communication features.
4. Operate authorized payment, subscription, or reward features.
5. Detect spam, fraud, harassment, impersonation, and other misuse.
6. Investigate user complaints and enforce our rules.
7. Provide customer assistance.
8. Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Services.
9. Send important account, security, and service notifications.
10. Comply with applicable legal obligations.
11. Process other information for purposes disclosed to you, with appropriate consent where required.

We will not use personal information for materially unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

## 4. Mobile Number Verification and WhatsApp OTP

HeartLoop may use third-party authentication and messaging providers, including the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta, to deliver one-time passwords (OTPs) for registration, login, account recovery, and security verification.

For this purpose, we may process and share limited information, such as:

- Your mobile phone number
- An authentication code or template parameter
- Message delivery identifiers and status
- Verification timestamps and related security records

This information is used to provide the authentication service, monitor delivery, and help prevent unauthorized access.

Authentication messages are intended for account verification and security, not unsolicited promotional messaging.

When WhatsApp is used to deliver an OTP, the relevant messaging provider may process information according to its own terms and privacy policy.

You can review WhatsApp's privacy information at:

https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy
